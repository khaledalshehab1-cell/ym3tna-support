  • للتذكير: تقدر توقفه داخل التطبيق أو من إعدادات إشعارات iOS.

  • لرصيد عملية معلّقة: استخدم تحديث عمليات الشراء داخل قسم الباقات، ولا تكرر الدفع لمعالجة المشكلة.
  • لتحديث العمليات: ما ينقل الرصيد بين الأجهزة ولا يعيد جلسات استُهلكت.
  • للاسترداد المالي: Apple تدير طلبات استرداد مشتريات App Store.

Yam3tna support

Email PRONOIA.Q8@GMAIL.COM with your device model, OS version, app version, and the steps leading to the problem.

For joining issues, use the same Wi-Fi network and keep the host app open. For purchase issues, check pending purchases in the app; do not repeat a purchase to troubleshoot. Apple manages App Store payment refunds. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or card details.

The trial or one credit is consumed when a valid game starts, even if the game ends early. Closing, force-quitting, or reopening does not automatically return it. A confirmed room fault returns a session only after the app successfully saves the return. Contact support if saving the balance fails.

If a refunded purchase leaves a balance adjustment for credits already used, new pack credits settle that adjustment first. The app shows the amount and the playable balance. Purchase refresh does not transfer credits to another device or restore consumed sessions.
